# Reconstruction of checkpoint cb05ef5

The user requested publication of local checkpoint
`cb05ef5f5b7ef5ada4e535e9dcc652eecaddc43e` to both `main` and
`verified-bipartite-continuation-2026-11-21`. The original complete checkout
and archive were unavailable. The user subsequently authorized recreating
the missing files. This publication is a new checkpoint with fresh proof
checking; it is not a byte-for-byte reproduction of the lost commit.

## What was recovered and what was recreated

The public baseline is `a825b24b0dd0ad6edd336388c740530bcfb38d99`. Nine later
Lean source files were recovered exactly: the full ordinary-factorization
wrapper, the exterior matching split, the coloring-to-factorization
conversion, and all six continuation modules listed in
[`few-exception-source.json`](few-exception-source.json). Their original
SHA256 hashes are fixed in
[`checkpoint-reconstruction-source.json`](checkpoint-reconstruction-source.json).

Eleven missing Lean modules were recreated: balanced paired-cut footprints;
the bounded cut/reserve, first-half and residual reduction; exterior color
classes; reserve label counting and choice; residual reserve transport;
selected coloring; balanced completion; and factorization from reserved
colors. The recovered public theorem statements and the nine recovered
source files are retained. The new internal proofs reuse the established
finite coloring, reserve repair and robust bipartite matching machinery.

The ordinary closure's complete current source inventory, source hashes,
public theorem names and source lines are in
[`ordinary-factorization-full-source.json`](ordinary-factorization-full-source.json).
The mapping includes fourteen modules: eleven recreated modules and three
recovered original modules. The six further continuation modules have their
own unchanged source mapping. Counts of declarations are computed by the
fresh audit; the historical counts are not substituted for current results.

## Verification and reproducibility

Run `python3 scripts/setup_vizing.py`, obtain the pinned mathlib cache, and
run `python3 scripts/verify.py`. The verifier checks dependency revisions,
reviewed source hashes, complete module reachability, a strict
`lake build --wfail`, and the unchanged stock `lake env lean Audit.lean`.
The audit permits only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
Every mapped public theorem must appear in its expected audited module;
private and generated proof declarations are included in the complete
declaration maps.

The files under `verification/` generated by that command describe the
current recreated sources. No historical successful build or axiom log is
used to certify them. `scripts/update_reconstruction_manifest.py` can
refresh mappings after a reviewed edit, but cannot refresh the fixed
recovered-original hashes and does not verify any proof.

The intended checked scope remains the recovered checkpoint's scope:
complete ordinary-core Lemma 4.2; the complete one-core branch of Corollary
4.6 with at most three exceptional vertices; terminal one-core
factorization under the recorded incidence hypotheses; and the exact
three-label safe-deletion criterion. This gives 47 complete numbered
assertions out of the unchanged 69-item inventory once the strict verifier
passes. The two-core branch of 4.6, full fixed-pattern Lemma 4.4, general
compatible-circuit selection, terminal Proposition 4.16, general Section 6
factorization, critical average-degree input, and the main theorem remain
incomplete.
